# Ḥadīth Citation Terminology

The short formulas that follow a ḥadīth in a book — **مُتَّفَقٌ عَلَيْهِ**, **رَوَاهُ البُخَارِيُّ فِي ...** — tell you who narrated it, where, and how authoritative it is. Reading them is itself a small grammar exercise.

---

## مُتَّفَقٌ عَلَيْهِ — Agreed Upon

!!! important "The Highest Certificate"
    **مُتَّفَقٌ عَلَيْهِ** means *agreed upon* — by both Imāms, **al-Bukhārī** and **Muslim**. Any ḥadīth in either Ṣaḥīḥ is known to be ṣaḥīḥ; a ḥadīth that both of them include is **a level beyond** — the highest authenticity certificate we have.

---

## رَوَاهُ ... فِي ...

> **رَوَاهُ البُخَارِيُّ فِي الجِهَادِ، وَمُسْلِمٌ فِي الإِمَارَةِ**

| Word | Role / Meaning |
|---|---|
| **رَوَى / يَرْوِي** | To narrate |
| **هُ** | **مَفْعُول بِه** — refers to the ḥadīth |
| **البُخَارِيُّ** | **فَاعِل** |
| **فِي الجِهَادِ** | In **كِتَاب الجِهَاد** — the chapter-book of his collection |
| **وَمُسْلِمٌ فِي الإِمَارَةِ** | And Muslim, in **كِتَاب الإِمَارَة** (leadership) |

The chapter reference is usually followed by the ḥadīth **numbers**.

---

## Example

> **لَغَدْوَةٌ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ أَوْ رَوْحَةٌ خَيْرٌ مِنَ الدُّنْيَا وَمَا فِيهَا** — مُتَّفَقٌ عَلَيْهِ
>
> *"A forenoon or an afternoon in the path of Allah is better than the world and whatever is in it."*

- **غَدْوَة** — the forenoon, before **زَوَال**; related: **غَدَاء** (modern: lunch; classical: breakfast)
- **رَوْحَة** — late afternoon / evening
- **لَ** — **لَام الاِبتِدَاء**. See [Lām al-Muzaḥlaqah](lam-muzaalaqa.md).
