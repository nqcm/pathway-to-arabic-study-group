# جَمْع مُنْتَهَى الجُمُوع — The Utmost Plural

Arabic can sometimes form a **plural of a plural**. But there comes a point beyond which **no further plural can be made**. That final plural is called **جَمْع مُنْتَهَى الجُمُوع** — *the plural of the utmost plurals*.

---

## The Plural of a Plural

Taking **بَيْت**:

| Word | Note |
|---|---|
| **بَيْت** | A house — **and also a couplet of poetry** |
| **بُيُوت** | Plural |
| **أَبْيَات** | Also a plural |

!!! question "An Open Question"
    Is **أَبْيَات** the plural of **بَيْت** in the sense of *couplets*, while **بُيُوت** is its plural in the sense of *houses*? Or is **أَبْيَات** a **جَمْع الجَمْع** — a plural of **بُيُوت**? The teacher was candid that she was unsure and undertook to check. Left as homework from Session 8.

---

## It Is Defined by the *Limit*, Not by Being a Plural of a Plural

!!! important "The Crucial Clarification"
    A student asked whether **جَمْع مُنْتَهَى الجُمُوع** necessarily means a plural formed *from* another plural, or simply the limit of pluralisation.

    **It is the limit.** It need not be built on another plural at all.

    **مَسَاجِد** proves the point: it is a plural of **مَسْجِد**, not of any other plural — yet you cannot form a further plural from **مَسَاجِد**. It is at the limit, and that is what qualifies it.

---

## Recognising It — the Patterns مَفَاعِل and مَفَاعِيل

You identify a **جَمْع مُنْتَهَى الجُمُوع** by its **pattern**: anything on the shape of **مَفَاعِل** or **مَفَاعِيل**, or resembling them.

!!! example "Ibn Mālik's Alfiyyah — the Rule of Prevention"
    > **وَكُنْ لِجَمْعٍ مُشْبِهٍ مَفَاعِلَا** ⁂ **أَوِ المَفَاعِيلَ بِمَنْعٍ كَافِلَا**
    >
    > *"And be, for a plural resembling* **مَفَاعِل** *or* **المَفَاعِيل**, *a guarantor of prevention."*

    **كَافِل** — guarantor, custodian, one who takes charge. The line instructs the student: *be the enforcer of* **مَنْع** *— prevention from tanwīn —* for any plural on these patterns.

!!! note "Poetic Licence"
    In **مَفَاعِلَا** the extra **أَلِف** is not part of the word; it is added for the metre. The actual pattern is **مَفَاعِل**.

---

## Consequence — Such Plurals Are Diptote

Every **جَمْع مُنْتَهَى الجُمُوع** is **مَمْنُوع مِن الصَّرْف** — it takes no tanwīn and is majrūr with a **فَتْحَة** — *unless* it is a **مُضَاف** or carries **ال**, in which case it takes a kasrah again.

| Example | Pattern | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **مَسَاجِد** | مَفَاعِل | **فِي مَسَاجِدَ** — fatḥah, being neither muḍāf nor with ال |
| **مَدَارِس** | مَفَاعِل | Kasrah when muḍāf — **مَدَارِسِ المَدِينَة** |
| **مَوَاقِع** | مَفَاعِل | **بِمَوَاقِعِ النُّجُومِ** — kasrah, because muḍāf |

> **فَلَا أُقْسِمُ بِمَوَاقِعِ النُّجُومِ** — *"So I swear by the setting places of the stars."*

For the full diptote rule and the second Alfiyyah couplet that states these two escapes, see [[mamnu-min-alsarf]].
