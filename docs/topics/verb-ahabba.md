# حَبَّ / أَحَبَّ — To Love

The root **ح-ب-ب** gives two verbs for *to love*: Form I **حَبَّ** and Form IV **أَحَبَّ**. In actual usage the two forms have **crossed over**: the verb comes from Form IV, but the maṣdar comes from Form I.

---

## Form I vs. Form IV

!!! note "From the Book (Surah Yusuf, Āyah 8)"
    > **وَهَذَا الفِعْلُ قَلِيلُ الاِسْتِعْمَالِ، وَكَثُرَ اسْتِعْمَالُ (أَحَبَّ) مِنْ بَابِ أَفْعَلَ**
    >
    > *This verb (حَبَّهُ) is seldom used; the usage of أَحَبَّ, from bāb afʿala (Form IV), is more frequent.*

| | Form I | Form IV |
|---|---|---|
| **Verb** | **حَبَّ** — hardly used | **أَحَبَّ / يُحِبُّ** — the common verb |
| **Maṣdar** | **حُبّ** — the common maṣdar | **إِحْبَاب** — almost never used |

!!! important "The Crossover"
    Use the **Form IV verb** with the **Form I maṣdar**. Whether you need the maṣdar as a **مَفْعُول مُطْلَق** or simply as a regular noun, it is **حُبّ**, not **إِحْبَاب**.

!!! tip "Why (أَحَبَّ) Is Not Declined in the Book"
    In **كَثُرَ اسْتِعْمَالُ (أَحَبَّ)**, **أَحَبَّ** is grammatically a **مُضَاف إِلَيه** — but it is quoted **as a word**, so it is left undeclined and put in brackets.

---

## Words for "Beloved"

All carry the sense of *one who is loved*, but usage differs:

| Word | Usage |
|---|---|
| **مَحْبُوب** | Usually the **romantic** kind of love |
| **حَبِيب** | **Affection** — parents, children, all can be called حَبِيب |
| **أَحِبَّاء** (pl.) | Used by the Jews of themselves: **نَحْنُ أَبْنَاءُ اللَّهِ وَأَحِبَّاؤُهُ** |
| **أَحَبُّ** | **اِسم تَفضِيل** — *more beloved* |

## Examples from the Quran

> **إِذْ قَالُوا لَيُوسُفُ وَأَخُوهُ أَحَبُّ إِلَىٰ أَبِينَا مِنَّا** (Yūsuf 8)
>
> *"When they said: Indeed Yūsuf and his brother are more beloved to our father than we are."*

> **وَتُحِبُّونَ الْمَالَ حُبًّا جَمًّا** (al-Fajr 20)
>
> *"And you love wealth with immense love."*

**تُحِبُّونَ** is Form IV (ḍammah on the prefix); its mafʿūl muṭlaq **حُبًّا** is the Form I maṣdar. See [Mafool Mutlaq](mafool-mutlaq.md).

> **نَحْنُ أَبْنَاءُ اللَّهِ وَأَحِبَّاؤُهُ** (al-Māʾidah 18)
