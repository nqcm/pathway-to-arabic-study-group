# Root ب-ر-ر (Barra) — Firm Land and Goodness

The root **ب-ر-ر** links two words that look unrelated in English, **بَرّ** (*land*) and **بِرّ** (*goodness*), through the idea of **stability**.

---

## بَرّ and بِرّ

| Arabic | Meaning | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **بَرّ** | Land | Something that **does not shake**, as opposed to the sea, where you are tossed about and your feet are not sure |
| **بِرّ** | Goodness | Because it is **stable** |

!!! tip "Good Is Stable"
    *"Good is always stable, evil is always shaky."* The teacher offered this as an instance of the wisdom of the ancient Arabs, reflected in their language.

## Examples from the Quran

> **فَلَمَّا نَجَّاهُمْ إِلَى الْبَرِّ إِذَا هُمْ يُشْرِكُونَ** (al-ʿAnkabūt 65)
>
> **فَلَمَّا نَجَّاهُمْ إِلَى الْبَرِّ فَمِنْهُم مُّقْتَصِدٌ** (Luqmān 32)

The sea voyage is also a metaphor: the firm land is the moment the turmoil of life is over, and we may go back to our old ways.
