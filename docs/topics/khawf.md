# خَوْف — Anticipating the Disliked

**خَوْف** has a broader meaning than simply being scared: it is anticipating something unpleasant, or the loss of something loved.

---

## The Definition

1. Anticipating **something disliked** (**مَكرُوه**).
2. Anticipating **the loss of something beloved** (**فَوَات مَحبُوب**).

## Forms

| Form | Word | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Form I | **خَافَ / يَخَافُ** | Transitive in itself |
| Maṣdar | **خَوْف**, **مَخَافَة** | The second is a [maṣdar mīmī](masdar-mimi.md) |
| Ism fāʿil | **خَائِف** | One who fears |
| Form IV | **أَخَافَ** | To make someone scared |
| Form IV ism fāʿil | **مُخِيف** | From **مُخْوِف**; see [Phonetic Harmony](phonetic-harmony.md) |

## Examples from the Quran

> **وَأَخَافُ أَن يَأْكُلَهُ الذِّئْبُ** (Yūsuf 13)

> **إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَآيَةً لِّمَنْ خَافَ عَذَابَ الْآخِرَةِ** (Hūd 103)
