# غَد — Tomorrow and Its Dropped Lām

**غَد** is the day that comes straight after the day you are talking about, on its heels (**عَقِب**).

---

## The Original Form

The original is **غَدْو**. The **لَام الكَلِمَة** — the third radical, **و** — has been dropped, leaving **غَد**, with tanwīn **غَدٌ**. In *lām al-kalimah*, "lām" refers to the third radical of the **فَعَل** template, not to an **ال**.

## In Poetry

> **إِنَّ مَعَ اليَوْمِ أَخَاهُ غَدْوَا**
>
> *"Indeed, with the day is its brother, tomorrow."*

The original form survives here, and the sense is like the English *tomorrow is another day*.

## Examples from the Quran

> **أَرْسِلْهُ مَعَنَا غَدًا** (Yūsuf 12) — **غَدًا** is a ẓarf of time, hence manṣūb.
