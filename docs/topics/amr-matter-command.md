# أَمْر — Matter and Command

**أَمْر** does not always mean a command. It often means a **matter** or condition, and the two meanings take different plurals.

---

## Two Meanings, Two Plurals

| أَمْر as | Plural |
|---|---|
| A **matter, condition** (glossed by **شَأْن**) | **أُمُور** |
| Something **ordained, a command** | **أَوَامِر** |

## شَأْن

**شَأْن** is difficult to translate into one English word; it often needs a few descriptive words. *The matter* is the easiest rendering.

## Examples from the Quran

> **لَتُنَبِّئَنَّهُم بِأَمْرِهِمْ هَٰذَا** (Yūsuf 15) — *You will surely inform them of **this matter** of theirs.* Not a command.

> **كُلَّ يَوْمٍ هُوَ فِي شَأْنٍ** (al-Raḥmān 29) — Every day He has a different matter, a different condition.
