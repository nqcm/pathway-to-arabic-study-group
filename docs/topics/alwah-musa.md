# الأَلوَاح — The Tablets of Mūsā

The **أَلْوَاح** are the slates on which the Torah was written, which Mūsā عليه السلام dropped in his anger on finding his people worshipping the golden calf.

---

## How the Torah Came

The Quran came down orally, with Jibrīl عليه السلام reciting it. That also happened with Mūsā عليه السلام, but the first bulk of the Torah on Ṭūr, as many traditions say, came **already written on stone slates from heaven**. Others say that Mūsā عليه السلام, who could read and write, wrote the waḥy down himself.

## Examples from the Quran

> **وَلَمَّا سَكَتَ عَن مُّوسَى الْغَضَبُ أَخَذَ الْأَلْوَاحَ ۖ وَفِي نُسْخَتِهَا هُدًى وَرَحْمَةٌ لِّلَّذِينَ هُمْ لِرَبِّهِمْ يَرْهَبُونَ** (al-Aʿrāf 154)

**سَكَتَ** here means *to go away, leave*: the anger left him. **لِرَبِّهِمْ يَرْهَبُونَ** is an example of [lām al-taqwiyah](lam-taqwiyah.md).
