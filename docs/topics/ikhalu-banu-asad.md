# إِخَالُ — The Banū Asad Kasrah

**إِخَالُ** (*I think*) is a first-person muḍāriʿ with a **kasrah** on its prefix — something standard Arabic never allows. It is the one surviving trace, in mainstream usage, of a feature of the **Banū Asad** dialect.

---

## The Anomaly

The first-person muḍāriʿ prefix **أ** normally takes:

| Verb type | Prefix vowel | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Three-letter māḍī (e.g. **كَتَبَ**) | **Fatḥah** | **أَكْتُبُ** |
| Four-letter māḍī (e.g. Form IV **أَنْزَلَ**) | **Ḍammah** | **أُنْزِلُ** |

**There is never a kasrah.** So why **إِخَالُ**?

---

## The Dialect of Banū Asad

!!! info "A Kasrah Marking a Kasrah"
    Verbs fall into families by the ḥarakah on the **second radical** of the māḍī — **كَتَبَ**, or **شَرِبَ** with a kasrah. In the dialect of the clan of **Banū Asad**, whenever the second radical had a **kasrah** in the māḍī, they put a **kasrah on the muḍāriʿ prefix**:

    | Standard | Banū Asad |
    |---|---|
    | **يَشْرَبُ** | **يِشْرَبُ** |

!!! note "Considered Inferior"
    This was regarded as a dialect feature, not classical enough. Literature generally avoided it, and other clans, who considered their own dialects superior, never used it.

!!! important "The Lone Survivor"
    Only **one** word with this kasrah was accepted into mainstream usage: **إِخَالُ**, from **خَالَ / يَخَالُ** (*to think*).

---

## Example from Poetry

> **وَمَا أَدْرِي وَسَوْفَ إِخَالُ أَدْرِي** ⁂ **أَقَوْمٌ آلُ حِصْنٍ أَمْ نِسَاءُ**

The teacher attributed this line to **Zuhayr ibn Abī Sulmā**. Note also **أَدْرِي** — Form I **دَرَى / يَدْرِي** (*to know*), unlike the Quran's frequent **وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ** — Form IV **أَدْرَى** (*to inform*). See [قَوْم](qawm.md).

!!! tip "Why Learn This?"
    It is not part of Sūrat Yūsuf. The point is the habit: **did you notice that إِخَالُ doesn't look like a normal verb, and ask why?** Train your mind to catch anomalies and then research them. See [Arabic Grammar Methodology](arabic-grammar-methodology.md).
