# مَعَ vs عِنْدَ — Company vs. Place

Both are **ẓarf** words that can translate as *with*, but **عِنْدَ** points to someone's place while **مَعَ** indicates only company.

---

## The Distinction

| Sentence | Implication |
|---|---|
| **كُنْتُ عِنْدَ مُحَمَّدٍ** | You went to where Muḥammad already was: his house, his office |
| **كُنْتُ مَعَ مُحَمَّدٍ** | Only the company; no indication of where it happened |

**مَعَ** is manṣūb as a ẓarf, and can be used adverbially: **خَرَجْنَا مَعًا** — *we left together*.

## Examples from the Quran

> **أَرْسِلْهُ مَعَنَا غَدًا** (Yūsuf 12) — **مَعَ** tells you the company.

> **لَا تَحْزَنْ إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَنَا** (al-Tawbah 40)
