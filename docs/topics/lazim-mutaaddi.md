# اللَّازِم وَالمُتَعَدِّي — Intransitive and Transitive Verbs

A **لَازِم** verb stops at its doer and does not take a direct object. A **مُتَعَدٍّ** verb passes its effect on to a mafʿūl bih.

---

## Making a Lāzim Verb Transitive

The book says of **ذَهَبَ** that it is **يُعَدَّى** (*made transitive*) in two ways.

| Means | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A preposition, **بِـ** | **أَن تَذْهَبُوا بِهِ** (Yūsuf 13) | That you take him away |
| A hamzah, **Form IV** | **أَذْهَبَ** | To take away |

Many verbs become transitive when brought to Form IV.

## Indirect Objects

**ذَهَبَ** is complete on its own, but a preposition can extend it: **ذَهَبَ إِلَى السُّوقِ**, **ذَهَبَ بِهِ**. What follows is a **مَفعُول بِهِ غَيرُ صَرِيح**. **سَمَحَ لَهُ** works the same way. These prepositions cannot be omitted (**ذَهَبَ السُّوقَ** is wrong), which separates them from [لَام التَّقوِيَة](lam-taqwiyah.md).

## The Bāb Can Decide

**حَزِنَ / يَحْزَنُ** is lāzim and **حَزَنَ / يَحْزُنُ** is mutaʿaddī. See [حَزِنَ / حَزَنَ](verb-hazana.md).

## Examples from the Quran

> **الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَذْهَبَ عَنَّا الْحَزَنَ** (Fāṭir 34) — Form IV

> **أَن تَذْهَبُوا بِهِ** (Yūsuf 13) — with **بِـ**
