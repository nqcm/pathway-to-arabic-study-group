# رَتَعَ — Grazing Freely, Relaxing

**رَتَعَ / يَرْتَعُ** (maṣdar **رَتْع**) first described cattle grazing however they wished, and then came to describe people relaxing without a care.

---

## From Cattle to People

**رَتَعَتِ المَاشِيَةُ** — the cattle grazed however they wanted. Picture a horse in a meadow with no bridle or reins, or cows and goats in a field, going wherever they like. From there the word was applied to humans spending carefree time: relaxing, rejuvenating.

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **مَاشِيَة** | Cattle, the ones who graze (distinct from **مَاشٍ**, a pedestrian) |
| **مَرْتَع** | A place to relax, a picnic spot |

## Examples from the Quran

> **أَرْسِلْهُ مَعَنَا غَدًا يَرْتَعْ وَيَلْعَبْ** (Yūsuf 12)
