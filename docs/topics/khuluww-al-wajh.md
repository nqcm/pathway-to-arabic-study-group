# يَخْلُ لَكُمْ وَجْهُ أَبِيكُمْ — The Idiom of an Empty Face

**خَلَا / يَخْلُو** means *to become empty*. Paired with **وَجْه** (face, countenance), it forms an idiom — **خُلُوُّ الوَجْهِ** — meaning **complete attention** or **complete affection**. It cannot be translated literally.

---

## The Verb خَلَا

**خَلَا / يَخْلُو / خُلُوّ** — a place or a bowl **becomes empty**: void of whatever was in it, which has been taken away.

---

## The Idiom

!!! important "Undivided Attention"
    **خُلُوُّ الوَجْهِ** is a **metaphor** for **complete attention** — and complete affection (**الْتِفَات**).

!!! example "The Phone"
    Your child comes to you while you are busy on your phone and you reply without looking up — that is **not** complete attention. Put the phone down and sit **facing** them — that is complete attention, and complete affection.

!!! example "The Book's Illustration"
    *I want to ask you about something, but so many people ask questions in class; may I visit you at home* **لِيَخْلُوَ لِي وَجْهُكَ** — *so that you can give me your complete attention.* Not *"so that your face becomes empty for me."*

    **لِيَخْلُوَ** is manṣūb because after this **لَام** there is always an implied **أَنْ**.

---

## Examples from the Quran

> **اقْتُلُوا يُوسُفَ أَوِ اطْرَحُوهُ أَرْضًا يَخْلُ لَكُمْ وَجْهُ أَبِيكُمْ وَتَكُونُوا مِن بَعْدِهِ قَوْمًا صَالِحِينَ** (Yūsuf 9)
>
> *"Kill Yūsuf or cast him out to [some] land; your father's countenance will [then] be only for you, and you will be after that a righteous people."*

The fāʿil of **يَخْلُ** is **وَجْهُ أَبِيكُمْ**. The brothers' reasoning: remove the objects of his affection whom he loves more than us, and his attention and affection will be ours alone.

!!! tip "Find the Fāʿil"
    A student's translation stumbled here. Identifying **وَجْهُ أَبِيكُمْ** as the fāʿil unlocks the meaning.
