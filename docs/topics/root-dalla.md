# Root ض-ل-ل (Ḍalla) — Straying and Not Returning

**ضَلَّ** is the verb of going astray. Its core sense is not merely *to wander off* but **to move away from the correct path and not come back to it** — not to be guided back. It is the direct opposite of **هُدَى**.

---

## Constructions and Maṣdars

| Arabic | Note |
|---|---|
| **ضَلَّ عَنِ الطَّرِيقِ** | With the preposition **عَنْ** — *he strayed from the way* |
| **ضَلَّ الطَّرِيقَ** | Directly transitive — both constructions are valid |
| **ضَلَال** | Maṣdar |
| **ضَلَالَة** | Also a maṣdar |
| **الضَّالّ / الضَّالِّين** | Ism fāʿil, and its sound masculine plural |

**ضَلَّ** is a **مُضَاعَف** (doubled/geminate) verb — see [[mudaaf-doubled-verbs]].

---

## Examples from the Quran

!!! example "سُورَة الفَاتِحَة"
    > **وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ**

    **الضَّالِّينَ** is the plural of **الضَّالّ**, formed as a **جَمْع مُذَكَّر سَالِم**.

!!! question "How Do We Know It Is Majrūr?"
    Not from the **فَتْحَة** visible on the nūn — that is not the case marker at all. In a sound masculine plural the sign is a **letter**:

    | Case | Sign |
    |---|---|
    | Marfūʿ | **وَاو** |
    | Manṣūb | **يَاء** |
    | Majrūr | **يَاء** |

    **الضَّالِّينَ** is **مَجرُور** as a **مَعطُوف** following **المَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ**, and its sign of jarr is the **يَاء**. See [[jama-muzakar-salim]].

!!! example "سُورَة يُوسُف — إِنَّ أَبَانَا لَفِي ضَلَالٍ مُّبِينٍ"
    > **إِذْ قَالُوا لَيُوسُفُ وَأَخُوهُ أَحَبُّ إِلَىٰ أَبِينَا مِنَّا وَنَحْنُ عُصْبَةٌ إِنَّ أَبَانَا لَفِي ضَلَالٍ مُّبِينٍ** (12:8)

---

## What the Brothers Meant — and Did Not Mean

!!! important "Not Religious Misguidance"
    A crucial point of tafsīr. **ضَلَال** in the brothers' speech is **not** deviation in matters of **دِين**. Yaʿqūb عليه السلام was a **prophet**; had they meant that he had gone astray in religion, their words would have amounted to kufr.

    What they meant was that he was **straying from the sensible course** — moving away from what logic, as they saw it, dictated: favouring **two** young children over **ten** grown, strong sons who were materially far more valuable to him. In their assessment, *"he is not thinking correctly."*

    **مُبِين** completes the accusation: *clearly* so, in their eyes. See [[mubeen]] and [[usba]].

!!! info "Attribution"
    Dr. V. Abdur Raheem is here paraphrasing this understanding from an earlier tafsīr rather than originating it.
