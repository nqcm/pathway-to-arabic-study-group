# خَسِرَ — To Sustain a Loss, To Be Destroyed

**خَسِرَ / يَخْسَرُ** is explained through trade: a merchant making a loss. Outside trade, the loss is of oneself.

---

## Two Usages

| Usage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| In the context of trade (**تِجَارَة**, **تَاجِر**) | To **sustain a loss** |
| Outside trade, said of a person | To **be destroyed**: not a material loss, but a loss to yourself |

## Three Maṣdars

Taught as three, with the words lost in the session captions; these are the ones that fit:

| Maṣdar | Note |
|---|---|
| **خَسَارَة** | The common one, used most of the time |
| **خُسْر** | Appears in the Quran |
| **خُسْرَان** | Appears in the Quran |

The teacher pointed to a book by **Dr. Fāḍil al-Sāmarrāʾī** on what difference it makes when different maṣdars of a verb are used, which discusses these at length.

## Examples from the Quran

> **قَالُوا لَئِنْ أَكَلَهُ الذِّئْبُ وَنَحْنُ عُصْبَةٌ إِنَّا إِذًا لَّخَاسِرُونَ** (Yūsuf 14)

> **وَإِن لَّمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ** (al-Aʿrāf 23)
