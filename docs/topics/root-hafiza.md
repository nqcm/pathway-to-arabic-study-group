# Root ح-ف-ظ (Ḥafiẓa) — Guarding and Memorising

**حَفِظَ** means *to guard, to protect*, and the same word is used for memorising the Quran. It takes a direct mafʿūl bih.

---

## Guarding the Quran

- **Al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī** said that when you memorise the Quran, it is not so much you protecting the Quran; rather, **the Quran is protecting you**.
- The aḥādīth tell us to guard the Quran because it is more prone to escape from our chests than a loosely tied camel, so it needs constant revision.

## Examples from the Quran

> **إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا الذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُ لَحَافِظُونَ** (al-Ḥijr 9)

> **وَإِنَّا لَهُ لَحَافِظُونَ** (Yūsuf 12) — the brothers' promise, with [lām al-taqwiyah](lam-taqwiyah.md) on **لَهُ** and the [muzaḥlaqah](lam-muzaalaqa.md) on **لَحَافِظُونَ**.
