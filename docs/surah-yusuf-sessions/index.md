# Surah Yusuf Study Sessions

Welcome to the study notes for Surah Yusuf. Below you will find the links to the individual study session notes, which cover the translation, tafsir, and lessons derived from this beautiful Surah.

## Study Sessions

- [Overview](surah-yusuf_overview.md) — Comprehensive reference: theme, context, narrative structure, parallels to Prophet ﷺ
- [Session 1](session-1.md) — Surah overview, context of revelation ('Ām al-Ḥuzn), parallels to Prophet ﷺ, the name Yusuf, mudāf iḍāfah
- [Session 2](session-2.md) — Ayah 1 vocabulary and derivatives, Nā'ib 'an al-Mafʿūl al-Muṭlaq, demonstrative pronoun تِلْكَ
- [Session 3](session-3.md) — Types of ال: al-ʿAhdiyyah (3 subtypes), al-Jinsiyyah (2 subtypes), al-Zā'idah; continuation of Āyah 1
- [Session 4](session-4.md) — Nawāsikh framework; af'āl nāqiṣah, af'āl muqārabah, af'āl qulūb; إنْ mukhaffafah; Āyah 2 tafsīr: ḥāl mumahhid, yāʾ al-nisbah, عقل etymology; Āyah 3 start: nūn al-ʿaẓamah, قصص etymology, ghaflah, waḥy
- [Session 5](session-5.md) — Nāʾib mafʿūl muṭlaq: adjective deputizing with three examples; إِنْ المُخَفَّفَة: لَام الفَارِقَة, four Quranic examples; Āyah 3 complete: mā maṣdariyyah; Āyah 4 begins: رَأَى root and Form IV أَرَى; celestial vocabulary (نَجم vs كَوكَب); يَا أَبَتِ nidāʾ; يُوسُفُ as diptote; compound numbers 11–19
- [Session 6](session-6.md) — Completing tamyīz rules across all number ranges; tafsīr of Dāwūd's sheep dispute; Āyah 4: هُمْ for inanimate stars/sun/moon and personification vs. "raising status," emphasis devices, رَأَى with two objects; جمع المذكر السالم conditions; Āyah 5 begins: لَا تَقْصُصْ, رُؤْيَا as diptote, إِخْوَة vs إِخْوَان, كَيْد and تَضمِين, عَدُوّ
- [Session 7](session-7.md) — Completing Āyah 5: عَدُوّ vs. حَذف المَوصُوف (النَّازِعَات، العَادِيَات), اِستِثنَاء مُنقَطِع in إِلَّا رَبَّ العَالَمِينَ, الإِنسَان as ال الجِنسِيَّة, مُبِين's لَازِم/مُتَعَدٍّ family, مَهِين homework, يَا بُنَيَّ diminutive and its five vocative forms; revisiting فَاء السَّبَبِيَّة and المُضَاعَف in the مَجزُوم
- [Session 8](session-8.md) — Āyah 6 complete: اِجتَبَى and اِستِخَارَة; الاِسم المَنقُوص / المَقصُور; تَأْوِيل and الأَحَادِيث; pronoun stacking and إِيَّا; كَذَٰلِكَ as نَائِب عَن المَفْعُول المُطْلَق; مِن التَّبعِيضِيَّة; قَبْلُ / بَعْدُ three-case rule; جَمْع مُنْتَهَى الجُمُوع and diptote escapes; صِيَغ المُبَالَغَة
- [Session 9](session-9.md) — التَّوَابِع and the four types of بَدَل; عَطْف النَّسَق and عَطْف البَيَان (two distinguishing rules); Āyah 7: آيَة as a desert sign, the three constructions of سَأَلَ, لَقَدْ and the omitted قَسَم, اِسم كَانَ vs فَاعِل, feminine agreement rules; Āyah 8 begins: لَام الاِبتِدَاء, عُصْبَة, ضَلَال
