# اِجْتَبَى — Choosing and Selection

**اِجْتَبَى / يَجْتَبِي** is a **Form VIII** verb from the root **ج-ب-ي**, meaning *to choose, to select, to single out*. It is the verb Yaʿqūb عليه السلام uses of Allah's selection of Yūsuf عليه السلام.

---

## The Derivational Family

| Arabic | Transliteration | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **اِجْتَبَى / يَجْتَبِي** | *ijtabā / yajtabī* | VIII | To choose, select |
| **اِجْتِبَاء** | *ijtibāʾ* | Maṣdar, VIII | Selection, choosing |
| **مُجْتَبٍ** | *mujtabin* | Ism fāʿil | The one who chooses |
| **مُجْتَبًى / المُجْتَبَى** | *mujtaban / al-mujtabā* | Ism mafʿūl | The chosen one |

---

## The Close Relative — اِصْطَفَى

**اِصْطَفَى** (root ص-ف-و) is also Form VIII and also means *to choose*. From it comes **المُصْطَفَى**, *the Chosen One* — a title of the Prophet ﷺ.

!!! important "No Two Words Are Perfectly Equal"
    Saying **اِجْتَبَى** "has the same meaning as" **اِصْطَفَى** does not make them 100% identical. In Arabic — as in most languages — no two words are exact equivalents; there are always subtle differences of nuance. For dictionary purposes they may be treated as synonyms, but the difference is real. This is a principle the teacher returns to repeatedly.

---

## Morphology Worth Noting

Both participles illustrate a phonetic pair that is easy to confuse:

- **مُجْتَبٍ** — the ism fāʿil, an **اِسْم مَنقُوص**: the final yāʾ is dropped and its tanwīn transfers back onto a kasrah, giving the double kasrah. See [[ism-manqus]].
- **مُجْتَبًى / المُجْتَبَى** — the ism mafʿūl, an **اِسْم مَقصُور** ending in **أَلِف مَقصُورَة**. See [[ism-maqsur]].

The same pairing holds for **مُصْطَفٍ** / **المُصْطَفَى**.

---

## Examples from the Quran

> **وَكَذَٰلِكَ يَجْتَبِيكَ رَبُّكَ** — *"And thus will your Lord choose you."* (Yūsuf 12:6)

Here the grammatical roles are: **يَجْتَبِي** the verb, **رَبُّكَ** the **فَاعِل** (the One who chooses), and **كَ** the **مَفْعُول** (the one chosen). Allah is **المُجْتَبِي**; Yūsuf عليه السلام is **المُجْتَبَى**.

The context matters: what Yaʿqūb عليه السلام means by **كَذَٰلِكَ** is *"just as Allah chose you for that dream — a dream itself pointing toward a lofty station — so too will He choose you for great matters,"* i.e. for prophethood. See [[mafool-mutlaq]] for the grammar of **كَذَٰلِكَ** here.
