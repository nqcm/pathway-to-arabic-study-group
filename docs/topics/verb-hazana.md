# حَزِنَ / حَزَنَ — To Be Sad, To Sadden

The root ح-ز-ن has two Form I verbs, and the bāb decides whether you are sad or are making someone else sad.

---

## The Two Verbs

| Verb | Bāb | Meaning | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **حَزِنَ / يَحْزَنُ** | **سَمِعَ** | To be sad | [لَازِم](lazim-mutaaddi.md) |
| **حَزَنَ / يَحْزُنُ** | **نَصَرَ** | To make someone sad | [مُتَعَدٍّ](lazim-mutaaddi.md) |

**حُزْن** is the opposite of happiness (**فَرَح**). The one who is sad is **حَزِين**.

## Examples from the Quran

> **لَا تَحْزَنْ إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَنَا** (al-Tawbah 40) — *Do not be sad.* Said in the cave of Thawr.

> **وَمَن كَفَرَ فَلَا يَحْزُنكَ كُفْرُهُ** (Luqmān 23) — *Let not his disbelief sadden you.*

> **إِنِّي لَيَحْزُنُنِي أَن تَذْهَبُوا بِهِ** (Yūsuf 13) — *It saddens me that you should take him.*

> **الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَذْهَبَ عَنَّا الْحَزَنَ** (Fāṭir 34)

## From Surah Yusuf Session 13 — Finding the Fāʿil

A student asked whether **يَحْزُنُنِي** could have a hidden **هُوَ** as its fāʿil. Because **حَزَنَ** is transitive, *something* is making him sad. Remove **أَن تَذْهَبُوا بِهِ** and the sentence is incomplete, since it does not say what. So the [maṣdar muʾawwal](masdar-muawwal.md) is the fāʿil, and the **ـِي** is the mafʿūl.
