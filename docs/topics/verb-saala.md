# سَأَلَ — Three Constructions of Asking

**سَأَلَ / يَسْأَلُ**, maṣdar **سُؤَال**, root **س-أ-ل**. Which of its three constructions is in play — and therefore which of its meanings — is signalled entirely by what follows the verb.

!!! note "Maṣdar or Ism Maṣdar?"
    Whether **سُؤَال** is a **مَصدَر** or an **اِسم مَصدَر** was left open in class — a fair uncertainty rather than a settled point. See [[ism-al-masdar]].

---

## 1. Two Objects — Asking *For* Something

**سَأَلَ** can take **two مَفعُول**: the **person asked** and the **thing requested**.

> **سَأَلْتُ المُدَرِّسَ كِتَابَ نَحوٍ سَهْلًا** — *"I asked the teacher for an easy grammar book."*

| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| **تُ** | فَاعِل |
| **المُدَرِّسَ** | مَفعُول 1 — the person |
| **كِتَابَ نَحوٍ سَهْلًا** | مَفعُول 2 — the thing requested |

!!! note "A مَصدَر مُؤَوَّل Can Fill the Second Slot"
    Wherever a maṣdar can stand, a **مَصدَر مُؤَوَّل** can stand:

    > **سَأَلْتُ المُدِيرَ أَنْ يَأْذَنَ لِي بِالسَّفَرِ**
    >
    > *"I asked the principal to give me permission to travel."* (**أَذِنَ** = to permit.)

    See [[masdar-muawwal]].

!!! example "سُورَة طه"
    > **وَأْمُرْ أَهْلَكَ بِالصَّلَاةِ وَاصْطَبِرْ عَلَيْهَا ۖ لَا نَسْأَلُكَ رِزْقًا ۖ نَّحْنُ نَرْزُقُكَ ۗ وَالْعَاقِبَةُ لِلتَّقْوَىٰ**

    **كَ** = mafʿūl 1; **رِزْقًا** = mafʿūl 2.

## 2. سَأَلَ عَنْ — Asking *About* Something

With **عَنْ** the meaning shifts from *wanting something from someone* to **seeking information** — to inquire.

> **نَسْأَلُ المُدِيرَ عَنِ الاِمْتِحَانِ مَتَى يَكُونُ**
>
> *"Let us ask the principal about the exam — when it will be."*

!!! question "The Grammar of مَتَى يَكُونُ"
    **مَتَى يَكُونُ** is a **بَدَل اِشْتِمَال** from **الاِمْتِحَان**: we are asking about the **timing**, which is connected to the exam but is neither a physical part of it nor the exam itself. See [[badal]].

!!! example "سُورَة المَائِدَة — a Weighty Āyah"
    > **يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَا تَسْأَلُوا عَنْ أَشْيَاءَ إِن تُبْدَ لَكُمْ تَسُؤْكُمْ وَإِن تَسْأَلُوا عَنْهَا حِينَ يُنَزَّلُ الْقُرْآنُ تُبْدَ لَكُمْ عَفَا اللَّهُ عَنْهَا ۗ وَاللَّهُ غَفُورٌ حَلِيمٌ**
    >
    > *"O you who believe, do not ask about things which, if disclosed to you, would trouble you. And if you ask about them while the Quran is being revealed, they will be disclosed to you. Allah has pardoned that, and Allah is Forgiving, Forbearing."*

    Even **before** this āyah was revealed, the Ṣaḥābah had the practice of hardly ever asking questions — for exactly this reason.

!!! info "The Cow of Banū Isrāʾīl"
    The cautionary parallel: a murder among Banū Isrāʾīl, and the people came to Mūsā عليه السلام to find the killer — leading to the command to slaughter a cow, the episode that gives **سُورَة البَقَرَة** its name.

    **Any** cow would have done. But they asked *what kind*, and a condition came; they asked the colour, and more conditions came. **With every question the task grew harder.** That is what al-Māʾidah 101 warns against: while revelation is ongoing, questions **will** be answered — and the answers will bind.

    The mercy in it: you would not like the disclosure, and Allah is withholding it from you to begin with.

## 3. سَأَلَ بِ — Same Sense as عَنْ

!!! example "سُورَة الفُرقَان، آية ٥٩"
    > **فَاسْأَلْ بِهِ خَبِيرًا** — *"So ask about Him one who is well-informed."*

!!! example "سُورَة المَعَارِج"
    > **سَأَلَ سَائِلٌ بِعَذَابٍ وَاقِعٍ** — *"A questioner asked about a punishment bound to fall."*

---

## Two Forms — سَأَلَ / سَالَ — and Two Imperatives

Both forms occur in the Quran.

!!! important "تَسْهِيل الهَمزَة"
    **سَالَ** arises from **سَأَلَ** by **easing the hamzah into an alif**. This is a purely **phonetic** change — nothing to do with grammar — but it propagates into every form built from the verb afterwards.

| Starting from | Muḍāriʿ | Steps | Imperative |
|---|---|---|---|
| **سَأَلَ** | **يَسْأَلُ** | drop the prefix; sukūn on the last letter | **اِسْأَلْ** |
| **سَالَ** | **يَسَالُ** | **تَسَالُ** → drop the prefix; sukūn on the last letter | **سَلْ** |

!!! question "Why the Alif Drops in سَلْ"
    Putting a sukūn on the last letter leaves the **alif** also carrying a sukūn — **two sukūns meeting**. The resolution is to drop the alif, giving **سَلْ**. See [[phonetic-harmony]].

!!! example "سَلْ — سُورَة البَقَرَة"
    > **سَلْ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ كَمْ آتَيْنَاهُم مِّنْ آيَةٍ بَيِّنَةٍ ۗ وَمَن يُبَدِّلْ نِعْمَةَ اللَّهِ مِن بَعْدِ مَا جَاءَتْهُ فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ شَدِيدُ الْعِقَابِ**

    **نِعْمَة** here is the understanding that came to them — which they then altered.

!!! example "سَلْهُمْ — سُورَة القَلَم"
    > **سَلْهُمْ أَيُّهُم بِذَٰلِكَ زَعِيمٌ**

    On **زَعِيم**: strictly a **صِفَة مُشَبَّهَة** rather than an ism fāʿil. And **زَعْم** covers a **spectrum** — it can describe someone claiming while **believing himself right** (and possibly being wrong), and equally someone who **genuinely is right**. The word alone does not settle which.

!!! example "اِسْأَلُوا After وَ or فَ"
    > **وَاسْأَلُوا اللَّهَ مِن فَضْلِهِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ كَانَ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عَلِيمًا** (al-Nisāʾ)
    >
    > **وَمَا أَرْسَلْنَا مِن قَبْلِكَ إِلَّا رِجَالًا نُّوحِي إِلَيْهِمْ ۚ فَاسْأَلُوا أَهْلَ الذِّكْرِ إِن كُنتُمْ لَا تَعْلَمُونَ** (al-Naḥl)

    The imperative is **اِسْأَلُوا**; when **وَاو** or **فَاء** precedes, the **hamzat al-waṣl** is not pronounced. See [[hamzatul-wasl]].

## السَّائِلِين

The ism fāʿil **سَائِل** (*one who asks*) gives the sound masculine plural **السَّائِلِين**, as in:

> **لَّقَدْ كَانَ فِي يُوسُفَ وَإِخْوَتِهِ آيَاتٌ لِّلسَّائِلِينَ** (Yūsuf 12:7)
