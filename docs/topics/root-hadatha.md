# حَدَثَ — Event, Newness, and Speech

The root **ح-د-ث** produces one of the most heavily used words in the Islamic sciences — **حَدِيث** — and its meaning did **not** begin with "speech."

---

## The Semantic History

| Stage | Arabic | Sense |
|---|---|---|
| Root verb | **حَدَثَ** | Something *took place* — an event, with an element of time in it |
| Adjectival | **حَدِيث** | *New* — e.g. **مُسْلِمٌ حَدِيثٌ**, "a new Muslim" |
| Extended | **حَدِيث** | The event that took place → the **account** of it |
| Final | **حَدِيث** | Any speech, any talk at all |

!!! important "The Order Matters"
    The meaning began with **event** — something that happens in time. Only afterwards did it come to mean the *report* of an event, and finally speech in general. This ordering is what explains the breadth of the technical term.

---

## The Arabic Definition

> **مَا يُتَحَدَّثُ بِهِ وَيُنْقَل**
>
> *"What is spoken about and transmitted."*

This is precisely why the term covers so much: the **speech** of the Prophet ﷺ, his **actions**, the occasions on which he **remained silent**, and the things he **prohibited** — all of it is **حَدِيث**, because all of it is what was talked about and passed on.

---

## أَحَادِيث — A سَمَاعِي Plural

The plural of **حَدِيث** is **أَحَادِيث**.

!!! note "No Rule Governs It"
    Normally a given noun pattern takes a predictable broken plural. **أَحَادِيث** is **سَمَاعِي** — *heard*, learned from usage rather than derived by rule. There is no pattern that dictates why **حَدِيث** should pluralise this way. It is simply what the Arabs said; language evolved organically first, and rules were extracted afterwards. (See the qiyāsī/samāʿī distinction in [[sighat-al-mubalaghah]].)

---

## Examples from the Quran

The Quran uses **حَدِيث** of **itself**:

!!! example "سُورَة المُرْسَلَات — the final āyah"
    > **فَبِأَيِّ حَدِيثٍ بَعْدَهُ يُؤْمِنُونَ**
    >
    > *"Then in what ḥadīth after this will they believe?"*

    *After this speech, what other speech is going to make them believe?* — **حَدِيث** here is the Quran.

!!! example "سُورَة الزُّمَر"
    > **اللَّهُ نَزَّلَ أَحْسَنَ الْحَدِيثِ**
    >
    > *"Allah has revealed the best of speech."*

And in Sūrat Yūsuf:

> **وَيُعَلِّمُكَ مِن تَأْوِيلِ الْأَحَادِيثِ** (12:6)

For what **تَأْوِيل الأَحَادِيث** means here, see [[tawil]].
