# لَهْو vs لَعِب — Two Kinds of Frivolity

**لَهْو** and **لَعِب** both describe useless, frivolous activity, and the Quran pairs them repeatedly to describe the life of this world. A distinction shared in class separates them by **who** typically does them and **what drives** them.

---

## The Distinction

| Word | Nature | Typical of |
|---|---|---|
| **لَعِب** | Baseless, frivolous play | **Children**, before puberty |
| **لَهْو** | Useless, frivolous things with **desires** attached | **Youngsters / teenagers** |

!!! note "Both at Any Age"
    Older people can, of course, exhibit **both** kinds of frivolity.

---

## Examples from the Quran

> **وَمَا هَٰذِهِ الْحَيَاةُ الدُّنْيَا إِلَّا لَهْوٌ وَلَعِبٌ ۚ وَإِنَّ الدَّارَ الْآخِرَةَ لَهِيَ الْحَيَوَانُ ۚ لَوْ كَانُوا يَعْلَمُونَ** (al-ʿAnkabūt 64)
>
> *"And this worldly life is nothing but diversion and play. And indeed, the Home of the Hereafter — that is the [true] life, if only they knew."*

!!! info "الحَيَوَان — The Contrast"
    Against **لَهْو** and **لَعِب**, the Hereafter is **الحَيَوَان** — the **ـَان** ending conveying life at its most intense: alive, active, throbbing, lasting forever. The teacher's image: this dunyā is like the **scale model** of a building in an estate agent's office — however closely it resembles the real building, it is not real and has no value compared to it. See [Sighat al-Mubalaghah](sighat-al-mubalaghah.md).

!!! example "Grammar in the Same Āyah"
    - **هَٰذِهِ الْحَيَاةُ** — a demonstrative followed by **ال**, so **الْحَيَاةُ** is a **بَدَل**
    - **لَهِيَ** — **lām al-muzaḥlaqah** on the **ḍamīr al-faṣl**. See [Lām al-Muzaḥlaqah](lam-muzaalaqa.md) and [Dameer al-Fasl](dameer-al-fasl.md).
