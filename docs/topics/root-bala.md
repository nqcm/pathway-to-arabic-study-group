# Root ب-ل-ي / ب-ل-و (Balā) — Wearing Out and Testing

**بَلِيَ / يَبْلَى** means *to wear out*; **بَلَا / يَبْلُو** means *to test*. Because some give the root as **ب-ل-و** and others as **ب-ل-ي**, most dictionaries **group the two words under one entry** — and the link between them is illuminating.

---

## The Two Verbs

| Verb | Meaning | Derivative |
|---|---|---|
| **بَلِيَ / يَبْلَى** | To wear out, decay, come to an end | — (the final yāʾ appears as **ى** in **يَبْلَى**) |
| **بَلَا / يَبْلُو** | To test, try | **اِبتِلَاء** — trial, test |

---

## From Wearing Out to Testing

!!! example "What Wearing Reveals"
    - A **garment** worn and washed over and over is wearing out — yet as it wears, its **stitches and weave** become more visible. What was inside comes out.
    - A **rock** worn by weather and water reveals what was **inside** it.

!!! important "The Abstract Meaning"
    The idea of **testing** grew from this. When a person is tested, **what was hidden inside comes out** — goodness or badness — it surfaces through **اِبتِلَاء**. Another instance of Arabic moving from the **physical** to the **abstract**.

---

## اِبتِلَاء vs اِمتِحَان

| Word | What is tested | How |
|---|---|---|
| **اِمتِحَان** | **Knowledge** | An examination |
| **اِبتِلَاء** | **Character** | Allah tests through hardship **or** ease, wealth **or** poverty |

!!! tip "Ease Is Also a Test"
    Wealth, health, and every good thing in life are **tests** too, and they bring out our inner nature. For some people goodness comes out when they are wealthy; for others, badness.

---

## Examples from the Quran

> **فَوَسْوَسَ إِلَيْهِ الشَّيْطَانُ قَالَ يَا آدَمُ هَلْ أَدُلُّكَ عَلَىٰ شَجَرَةِ الْخُلْدِ وَمُلْكٍ لَّا يَبْلَىٰ** (Ṭāhā 120)
>
> *"Then Shayṭān whispered to him; he said: 'O Ādam, shall I direct you to the tree of eternity and a kingdom that will not wear out?'"*

- **لَا يَبْلَىٰ** — a sentence functioning as **نَعت** for the indefinite **مُلْكٍ**.

!!! info "Shayṭān's Strategy"
    The promise of a kingdom **that never wears out** targets a desire Allah created in every human being — the longing for permanence, youth, and good times that never end (today a multi-billion-dollar anti-ageing industry). Shayṭān understands our psychology and attacks from our **weak points**, beginning with something small, not with blatant kufr or shirk.
