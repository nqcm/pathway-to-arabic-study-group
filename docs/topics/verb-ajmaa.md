# أَجْمَعَ — To Resolve, To Agree

**أَجْمَعَ / يُجْمِعُ** (Form IV, root ج-م-ع) means to be firm on a decision. Used of a group, it adds the meaning of all agreeing.

---

## Construction

| Construction | Form |
|---|---|
| Direct mafʿūl | **أَجْمَعُوا أَن يَجْعَلُوهُ** — the [maṣdar muʾawwal](masdar-muawwal.md) is the mafʿūl |
| With **عَلَى** | The other permitted construction |

## Examples from the Quran

> **فَلَمَّا ذَهَبُوا بِهِ وَأَجْمَعُوا أَن يَجْعَلُوهُ فِي غَيَابَتِ الْجُبِّ** (Yūsuf 15)

In the previous āyāt, one brother suggested one thing and another something else. By this āyah, they have **all agreed**. The plural carries both meanings: deciding, and agreeing.

## In the Ḥadīth on Fasting

> **مَنْ لَمْ يُجْمِعِ الصِّيَامَ قَبْلَ الْفَجْرِ فَلَا صِيَامَ لَهُ**
>
> *"Whoever has not resolved upon the fast before Fajr, there is no fast for him."*

- This is for **farḍ** fasts: you must be decided in your heart beforehand. Someone who already intended to fast, and who wakes after Fajr, has a valid fast. Someone undecided until after Fajr has begun does not.
- For **nafl** fasts, the intention can be made during the day as long as the person has not eaten or drunk anything.
