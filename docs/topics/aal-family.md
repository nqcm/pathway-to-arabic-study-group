# آل — Family, Folk, Followers

**آل** is a noun whose range runs from the narrowest domestic sense outward to a whole community. Which sense is meant is settled entirely by context.

---

## The Range of Meaning

| Sense | Note |
|---|---|
| The people **close** to a person | The core meaning |
| The **immediate family** | The most common use |
| **Followers** / adherents | E.g. of a leader or a teacher |
| A **community** | The widest sense |

---

## Examples from the Quran

> **وَيُتِمُّ نِعْمَتَهُ عَلَيْكَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ يَعْقُوبَ** — *"…and complete His favour upon you and upon the family of Yaʿqūb."* (Yūsuf 12:6)

!!! note "In آلِ يَعْقُوبَ"
    Here **آل** *does* mean the immediate family of Yaʿqūb عليه السلام — but the word also extends downward along the line: to the **descendants** further on, grandchildren and great-grandchildren included.

---

## Related — أَبَوَيْكَ, the "Two Fathers"

The same āyah continues **عَلَىٰ أَبَوَيْكَ مِن قَبْلُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَإِسْحَاقَ**.

**أَبَوَيْكَ** is the **dual** of **أَب**:

- The independent dual is **أَبَوَانِ**;
- In the manṣūb/majrūr state it is **أَبَوَيْنِ**;
- As a **مُضَاف** (to **كَ**) the **نُون** drops → **أَبَوَيْ + كَ = أَبَوَيْكَ**. See [[mudaf-idafah]].

!!! important "Not Father and Grandfather"
    The āyah names them: **إِبْرَاهِيم** and **إِسْحَاق**. Relative to Yūsuf عليه السلام these are his **grandfather** (Isḥāq) and **great-grandfather** (Ibrāhīm). Arabic uses **أَب** freely for ancestors up the line, not only for the immediate father.

For the iʿrāb of **إِبْرَاهِيمَ** as a majrūr **بَدَل** carrying a fatḥah, see [[mamnu-min-alsarf]] and [[badal]].
