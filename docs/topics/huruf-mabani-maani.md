# حُرُوف المَبَانِي وَالمَعَانِي — Letters and Particles

Where English has two words, *letters* and *particles*, Arabic has one: **الحُرُوف**. Grammar then separates the letters that build words from the particles that carry or alter meaning.

---

## The Two Kinds of Ḥarf

| Term | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **حُرُوف المَبَانِي** | The letters of the alphabet | The **building blocks** of words, from **بَنَى / يَبْنِي** (*to build*) |
| **حُرُوف المَعَانِي** | Particles | They **contain a meaning themselves** or **alter the meaning of something else** |

!!! example "Altering Meaning"
    *I am writing* becomes *I am not writing* by placing **لَا** before the verb.

## Working and Non-Working Particles

There are **more than 30 kinds** of ḥurūf al-maʿānī, grouped into two.

### الحُرُوف العَامِلَة

These have some effect on the **endings of their مَعمُول**:

| Family | Effect |
|---|---|
| **حُرُوف الجَرّ** | The maʿmūl is **majrūr** |
| The particles of naṣb | Give **naṣb** to the muḍāriʿ |
| The particles of jazm (**لَمْ**, **لَمَّا** and the like) | Give **jazm** to the muḍāriʿ |
| **الحُرُوف المُشَبَّهَة بِالفِعل** (**إِنَّ**, **أَنَّ** and the like) | Make a noun behave like the object of a verb |

### Non-Working Particles

These have **no maʿmūl** and change no endings. The usual names are **غَيْر عَامِلَة** or **مُهْمَلَة**.

!!! important "Grammatical Effect, Not Effect on Meaning"
    "No effect" refers only to **iʿrāb**. A non-working particle can still change the meaning, as the lām of emphasis does.

## One Ḥarf, Several Categories

The categories overlap. **The lām** can be a ḥarf jarr, can give jazm, and is listed by some as giving naṣb. See [A Map of the Lāms](lam-types.md). A ḥarf can also change category with what follows it: [إِذَن](idhan-particle.md) works only on a muḍāriʿ and has no effect on a noun.
